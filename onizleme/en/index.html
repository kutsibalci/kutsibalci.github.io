HeatShield

An agent that uses the mobile network as its sensor to protect construction workers from heat stress.

In Qatar, outdoor work must stop once heat passes the legal limit. HeatShield detects workers still on site through the network, with no extra device, spends its query budget on the workers most at risk first, and writes every decision to a record that can be used in an inspection. I built it on my own in two and a half weeks and presented it live to an international jury.

ArrivalGuard

An agent that keeps medical tourists safe as they're picked up at the airport, driven by mobile network signals.

It never tracks the patient's location; it decides from network events: arrival in the country, whether the SIM was recently swapped, whether the phone is reachable, and entry into an area. It verifies the driver through the driver's own phone, warns against fake "clinic driver" calls and records the reasoning behind every decision. The same signal (the patient can't be reached) means an alarm before they've met the driver, and most likely a local SIM card afterwards; the system knows the difference.

Remote Connect (Uzaktan Bağlantı)

A remote desktop that lets you control a Windows PC from your phone's browser, with nothing to install.

Like TeamViewer, but with no cloud server: video, audio and files go straight from the computer to the phone. The screen is compressed to H.264 by the graphics card's hardware encoder and decoded in the browser. It has file transfer, a two-way clipboard, an audio mixer, a remote-control mode and HTTPS on the home network with its own certificate.

EV Rota

An electric vehicle charging planner that accounts for whether a station actually works.

A charger on the map may be broken or busy. EV Rota gives every station a reliability score from driver reports, builds the plan around that score and shows a backup station for every stop, checked by real road distance. Its energy estimate accounts for hills, temperature, wind and speed.

A bookkeeping program for trucking businesses that keeps trips, vehicles, fuel, customer accounts and invoices in one place.
