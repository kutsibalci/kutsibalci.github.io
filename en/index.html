HeatShield

An AI agent that uses the mobile network as its sensor to protect construction workers from heat stress.

In Qatar, outdoor work must stop once heat passes the legal limit. HeatShield detects workers still on site through the network, with no extra device, spends its query budget on the workers most at risk first, and writes every decision to a record that can be used in an inspection. I built it on my own in two and a half weeks and presented it live to an international jury.

ArrivalGuard

An agent that keeps medical tourists safe as they're picked up at the airport, driven by mobile network signals.

It never tracks the patient's location; it decides from network events: arrival in the country, whether the SIM was recently swapped, whether the phone is reachable, and entry into an area. It verifies the driver through the driver's own phone, warns against fake "clinic driver" calls and records the reasoning behind every decision. For example, if the patient can't be reached before meeting the driver, that's an alarm; afterwards, it most likely means they've switched to a local SIM card. The system tells the two apart.

Remote Connect (Uzaktan Bağlantı)

A remote desktop that lets you control a Windows PC from your phone's browser, with nothing to install.

Like TeamViewer, but with no cloud server: video, audio and files go straight from the computer to the phone. The screen is compressed to H.264 by the graphics card's hardware encoder and decoded in the browser. It has file transfer, a two-way clipboard, an audio mixer, a remote-control mode and encrypted HTTPS connections on the home network using its own certificate.

Logistics Accounting

A bookkeeping program for trucking businesses that keeps trips, vehicles, fuel, customer accounts and invoices in one place.

Fuel, tolls and driver allowances are subtracted from each trip's income to show its net profit. Vehicle and driver document deadlines are tracked with warnings, e-invoices are imported from files, and Turkish tax withholding rules are checked automatically. It runs from a folder with no installation.
